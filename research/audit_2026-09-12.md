# Platform audit - 2026-09-12 (operator)

Six independent read-only reviews (live runners, research harness and intraday strategies, data
pipelines, dashboard, daily LEAN champion, ML/options/futures sweeps) plus operator spot checks
of every P0 against the code and the state files. Nothing was edited during the review. The
unit suite (`tests/`, 107 tests) passes. Findings are ordered by what they can cost, with the
owning track in brackets; fix them through the normal gates (`--replay`, `compare_orders.py`,
`tests/`) and record each in the track journal. The backlog carries the same items as AUD-xx.

## What is clean (verified, not assumed)

- No look-ahead in `algorithms/intraday/base.py` or any intraday `signal.py`; every feature uses
  bars at or before t; the harness decides on close and fills at the next open with signed
  commission.
- LEAN daily path: decision on D's close, fill at the D+1 open, confirmed from the engine log; the
  signal is window-length invariant (307 vs 502 bars give identical weights); the factor-file
  pipeline reproduces LEAN's lookup and agrees with Yahoo `Adj Close` to about 1e-7.
- Both minute stores are tz-aware UTC on disk and ET on load, DST verified; IBKR and Alpaca closes
  agree to about 1e-6 over 101k bars per symbol; no duplicate, NaN or zero-volume bars.
- F-1's t +4.74 survives Newey-West (11 and 55 lags), day clustering and a 2,000-rep day-block
  bootstrap.
- Options sweeps price the correct side of the quote at entry and exit; strikes come from the
  entry snapshot only.
- Dashboard: no key value appears in any served file; the `secret` guard is not bypassable
  (3x decode, case, raw path + query); no order-placing call exists; binds 127.0.0.1 only.

## P0 - can place wrong orders, leave risk open, or liquidate the book

### AUD-01 [ops] Trading tasks stop on battery
`install_intraday_task.ps1:19`, `install_paper_task.ps1:24`. Both trading tasks were registered
with StopIfGoingOnBatteries and DisallowStartIfOnBatteries, and this machine is a laptop with a
battery. Unplug at 11:00: the intraday process tree is killed with positions open, no flatten, no
alert; the 15:45 rebalance refuses to start. The installers now pass
`-AllowStartIfOnBatteries -DontStopIfGoingOnBatteries -StartWhenAvailable`; the registered tasks
pick that up only when the installers are re-run.

### AUD-02 [eng] Zero NAV liquidates the book
`paper_trade.py:411`: `net_liq = float(summary.get("NetLiquidation", 0) or 0)` has no floor. An
empty `accountSummary()` (Gateway mid-reset, slow sync) makes every target 0 shares and
`plan_orders` sells the whole book; the margin check is weight-based and passes. Fix: exit 3 and
notify if `net_liq <= 0` or it moved more than 50% against `last_run.json["equity"]`.

### AUD-03 [eng] Foreign filter drops legacy holdings
`paper_trade.py:493-497`. When any intraday-universe position exists at 15:45, `positions` is
filtered to `s in universe`, which also drops legacy holdings such as the TQQQ orphan. The S-18
orphan fix therefore only works on days the intraday sleeve is perfectly flat, which is exactly
not the days AUD-06 and AUD-07 bite. Fix: `if s not in _INTRADAY_UNIVERSE`, unconditionally,
with a unit test.

### AUD-04 [eng] Mock runs poison the live state file
`paper_trade.py:506-522`. `--mock` and `--dry-run` write `live/state/last_run.json` and append
`MockAccount.net_liq=100000` to the live equity curve; `held_age` counts runs, not sessions. The
live file today holds `equity 100000`, `peak 100000`, `equity_curve [100000 x4]` and `held_age`
15 after seven runs on one signal date. Harmless only because the champion runs `DEFAULTS` with
the drawdown breaker off; enabling the breaker would read a 90% drawdown and liquidate. Fix:
`save_state` only on real paper runs, mock state to a separate file, `held_age` per signal date.

### AUD-05 [eng] Missing bar marks a position at $0
`intraday_trader.py:85,378`. `Book.pnl` uses `prices.get(s, 0.0)`. Yahoo drops SMCI for one
minute: a $30k long reads as a $30k loss, exceeds the 2.5% limit, the whole book is flattened and
the day is over. `tests/test_intraday_book.py:82-86` enshrines this behaviour. Fix: keep
`last_px` per symbol in `Trader`; exclude unmarked symbols from the limit and alert.

### AUD-06 [eng] In-flight orders are invisible
`intraday_trader.py:319-333,368-369`. Sizing and the flatten read `book.pos` only; `settle()`
books an order only when complete and holds "Cancelled"-labelled orders for 90 s. The same delta
is resent every minute until booked: a slow fill at 15:38 becomes a double sell at 15:39 and a
buy-back at 15:40; a genuinely rejected order is resent for 90 s. Fix: net pending signed
quantity from `ex.open` into `cur`; skip symbols with open orders in the flatten branch; make the
test `FakeExecutor` settle lazily so this path is covered.

### AUD-07 [eng+iterate] No exchange calendar anywhere
`intraday_common.py:62`, `intraday_trader.py:240,336-357`, `intraday_backtest.py:251-258`,
`alpaca_data.py:100-102`. `FLATTEN_MINUTE=368` is unreachable on a 13:00 close (210 bars). Live:
the RTH feed stops at 12:59, `step()` returns on `t <= last_t`, no flatten is ever sent, and the
position is carried into the 15:45 rebalance as "foreign". Harness: silently closes at the 12:59
close price with zero latency. Alpaca store: all 21 early closes 2016-2025 carry 13:00-15:59
after-hours bars, so `late_momo` opens at 15:00 in the post-market tape and the flatten fills
against it at 1.5 bp. Holidays: both tasks fire, and the daily runner queues MKT orders for the
next open on a stale signal. Next early close is 2026-11-27. Fix: an early-close and holiday
calendar (hard-coded list or `pandas_market_calendars`) in `load_bars`, `alpaca_data`, both
launchers and the flatten minute (close minus 22); flatten on wall clock when bars stop
advancing; make the harness count and fail on "eod" fills.

### AUD-08 [eng] Book and account can diverge
`intraday_trader.py:461-479,537-542`, `paper_trade.py:448-470`. Start-up and `--flatten` sell
what the book says, not what the account holds; the daily runner's HALT path sells intraday names
the intraday book still owns; orders sent outside RTH (`outsideRth=False`) queue to the next open
with nothing reconciling them. A position closed by any other path becomes an untracked short at
the next open, and the sleeve then re-sells it. Fix: reconcile the book against `ib.positions()`
and cancel open `orderRef=INTRADAY` orders at start-up (make `--flatten-from-account` the
default); apply the foreign rule on the daily HALT path; refuse submits outside RTH and alert.

### AUD-09 [eng] Feed, crash and ordering defects
`intraday_trader.py:289-303,500-511,325,352`, `paper_trade.py:221,345`. `--feed auto` probes
delay at 09:25 against yesterday's 15:59 bar, so it is always Yahoo and never falls back to IB; IB
streams are not re-subscribed after reconnect; a yfinance outage raises `KeyError` before any log
or alert; `notify()` (up to 45 s) runs before the loss-limit flatten submit; a NaN price passes
`if not px` and `math.floor` raises, skipping the whole step including the flatten. Fix: probe
after 09:31 or against the prior close; rebuild the feed on reconnect; wrap `main()` with
`log_event("crashed")` plus notify; submit first, notify second; `math.isfinite`.

## P1 - research results or promotions can be wrong

### AUD-10 [daily+critic] Promotion leaves the old champion's numbers in place
`evaluate.py:208-213`, `champion_stats():88-96`. `--promote` never rewrites `stats_by_spread`,
and `champion_stats` prefers that column. The next candidate is judged against the previous
champion's numbers, so a 25.0% run can be promoted over a 26.0% champion. It has not bitten
because the file was hand-edited. Fix: on promote, write the run's column and delete the others;
refuse when `columns[key].run_dir != run_dir`.

### AUD-11 [daily] The OOS label is in-sample
`sweep_s1.py:10,33-34`, the `Params` docstring in `signals.py`, the OOS notes in `champion.json`.
Every shipped parameter (`mom_skip`, `alloc_vol_window`, the 252 shelf, the band) was chosen on
full-period tables; the 2020-2026 "OOS" is a sub-period check of the chosen set. The honest
~20% CAR is fully in-sample, and the 33% PSR says the same. Every OOS-weighted claim (S-18,
S-22, S-26, S-28) inherits it. Fix: re-select on 2012-2019 and report 2020-2026 once, or relabel
and treat paper trading as the only out-of-sample evidence.

### AUD-12 [daily+eng] The runner ignores parameter overrides
`paper_trade.py:196`, `compare_orders.py:158`, `evaluate.py:99-125,204-214`. The runner passes
`getattr(sig, "PARAMS", None)`; `signals.py` defines `DEFAULTS`, so the runner always trades
`Params()`; `--promote` stores no `env`; the gate uses `Params()` too. A run promoted with any
`S1_*` override would pass the gate while paper trades the defaults. Fix: refuse promotion when
`run["env"]` has non-whitelisted keys, or build `Params` from `champion.json["env"]` in both the
runner and the gate.

### AUD-13 [daily+eng] No data-completeness gate
`paper_trade.py:502-505`, `signals.py:884,976`. A missing SPY column yields `regime_reason
"SPY missing"` and flattens everything; an all-NaN column silently rotates XLK to QQQ; a last-row
NaN is forward-filled and sized on a stale price. A yfinance outage at 15:45 is a full round trip
at real spread. Fix: exit 3 and notify if any universe column is missing, the last row has NaN,
or `as_of` is older than the previous session; never flatten on a reason that names a missing
ticker.

### AUD-14 [eng+critic] Promotion tooling gaps
`evaluate.py:127-162`, `intraday_backtest.py:325-327`, `backtest.py:33-53`. No track guard (an
`intraday/<x>` row is comparable to the LEAN champion); intraday rows record `commit ""` (562 of
735 rows); `OrderListHash` is not captured into the ledger, so the champion's "must reproduce"
note is unverifiable by tooling; dirty trees are not flagged. Fix: refuse cross-track promotion;
record `git rev-parse --short HEAD` plus a dirty flag in both recorders; parse `OrderListHash`
from the engine output into `stats` and require it on promote.

### AUD-15 [data] The IBKR minute store is split-adjusted, the code assumes raw
`intraday_common.py:66-100`, `intraday_backtest.py:107`. IBKR closes match Alpaca to 1e-6
through SOXS's 2026-03-05 and 2026-07-15 reverse splits and NFLX's 10:1, so the store is on the
current share basis throughout, yet `data/minute/_splits.json` does not exist and `share_scale`
is 1.0. Per-share commission on SOXS before 2026-03-05 is modelled at 1/200 of reality (about
1.25 bp versus about 100 bp per side for ~130 sessions), NFLX 10x for ~58 sessions, in every
IBKR-store backtest; an incremental fetch after a future split would mix bases silently. Fix:
write `_splits.json` for `data/minute` (the derivation is source-agnostic) or fall back to the
Alpaca table; in `save_bars` refuse a merge whose overlapping closes disagree.

### AUD-16 [data] The IBKR fetcher cannot extend and truncates today
`intraday_data.py:56-58,156`. `snap_after_close` returns `min(20:00, now)`, and `fetch_symbol`
skips any window with 15 or more sessions on disk. All 16 symbols hold a 170-175-bar 2026-09-11
(written 12:35 ET); `--months N` is a no-op; `--repair` cannot add missing days. Fix: snap to the
previous 20:00 during RTH; fetch when any session is missing or short; make the default run
include repair.

### AUD-17 [data] LEAN daily store is adjusted prices labelled raw
`fetch_data.py:19-24,141,165`. Split-adjusted prices are written as raw with `split_factor=1`.
Only `ADJUSTED` mode is valid (all algorithms use it); LEAN's IB fee model then charges $0.005 on
adjusted share counts, so pre-2018 TQQQ/UPRO orders hit the 0.5% cap (conservative for the
champion) and SQQQ/SOXS are understated; `sweep_s19.py:76` calibrates with a 1% cap while
LEAN's is 0.5%. Fix: store true raw prices with real split factors; align the sweep's cap.

### AUD-18 [ml] F-3's t-stat is naive on overlapping labels
`sweep_f3.py:179,291,566`. 5-day labels overlap; the IC t-stat is iid over dates; no purge or
embargo between the training and early-stopping years. Naive t +2.17 becomes Newey-West(5)
+1.27 and NW(21) +1.18; the journal's "IC +0.011 wherever pointed" is wrong at the daily horizon
(the refusal stands). Fix: HAC t with lag = h, or thin to every h-th date; embargo h sessions.

### AUD-19 [ml] F-7 draft selects on the test window
`ml_f7.py:394-420,501-505,662` (uncommitted draft). The persistence table divides an 11-interval
IC measured only on the 09:55 slot by the pooled IC(1); the best of 23 correlated cells is chosen
on the 2024-26 test window; the 2019-26 extension includes the selection years. Per-slot IC(1)
at 09:55 is -0.002 (t -0.25); backing 2024-26 out gives about -$620/day over 2019-23, the truly
out-of-sample years. Fix: compute IC(1) on the identical timestamp subset per h; report 2019-23
separately; treat 2024-26 as in-sample for construction choice.

### AUD-20 [ml] F-1 shifts by row on an irregular grid
`sweep_f1.py:128,202-203`. `diff`/`shift` operate on a 5-minute grid with empty bins dropped.
SOXS has missing bins in 16.6% of sessions (mean 1.6, max 33), so its horizons and features differ
from the rest; `r6`/`r12` at 09:55 and 10:25 cross the overnight boundary. Fix: reindex each
session to the 78-bin grid before shifting, as `sweep_x1._panel_job` does.

### AUD-21 [iterate] Harness biases, all in its own favour
`intraday_backtest.py:211-218,226,280-303,377`, `base.py:50`. Missing-bar fills execute at the
decision bar's close with zero latency; the live loss limit uses account NAV while the harness
uses sleeve equity (live is 1/`equity_frac` looser); turnover divides by `equity0`; drawdown is
end-of-day only; `--split` records `end` rather than `split-1`; true range at bar 0 includes the
overnight gap, inflating `atr14` for minutes 4-13. Fix: hold the order to the next real bar; the
same equity base in both; group the true-range shift by day.

## P2 - dashboard, ops, data hygiene

### AUD-22 [eng] Dashboard
`reqAllOpenOrders` and `reqExecutions` run only at (re)connect, so orders placed by clients 71
and 17 afterwards never show while `as_of` advances (set the Gateway's Master API client ID to 81
or re-request each tick). `nav_history.jsonl` appends every 60 s around the clock including
identical rows and is fully materialised per request. No `TrustedHostMiddleware`: `Host:
evil.example.com` returns 200, so a DNS-rebinding page can read account and positions. A failed
NAV append tears down the IB session. `/api/logs` is the one path not passed through `redact()`.
Chart.js loads from a CDN without SRI. The task pins Python 3.14. `/api/options?symbol=` sets a
global for every viewer. Do not expose this server publicly; if snapshots are wanted, write a
separate whitelisting exporter.

### AUD-23 [ops] Gateway died silently twice
At 10:47 and 10:54 ET the OpenClaw gateway node process died with no log line and no Windows
crash event while the scheduled task still reported "Running", because its wscript/cmd wrappers
outlived node and Task Scheduler's restart-on-failure never fired; the task-supervisor's Job
Object then killed every running research session (four jobs marked "interrupted by gateway
restart"). `scripts/gateway_watchdog.ps1` (task "OpenClaw Gateway Watchdog", every 5 minutes)
now restarts it. Root cause not found; if it recurs under load, stagger the job cadences.

### AUD-24 [data] Store hygiene
`events.py` overwrites `earnings.json` with a near-empty calendar on HTTP 402 (7 of 16 sleeve
names have no date). Partial option caches are marked complete forever (`odte/SPY/2026-09-10`
ends 15:35; the `raw/*_front` files stop at 09-09). `iv_regime` has 6-day holes in holiday weeks
(Thursday expirations) and row-shifts across them. Alpaca's winter end-clamp truncates the newest
session's last hour. ib_async returns `[]` on IBKR error 162, so a lost month is invisible.
`save_bars` uses one fixed `.tmp` and no lock. `minute_manifest.json` is rewritten whole and
records SPY only. Neither minute store's 15:59 bar carries the closing auction print; use
`data/auctions` for close marks. The futures stitch is not back-adjusted and rolls mid-session.

### AUD-25 [daily] Signal-module loose ends
`ML_MODE="rank"` gates on the forecast's sign contrary to its docstring (S-27's ranked books were
gated on two signs). `Params.__post_init__` guards only the momentum window: a long
`trend_window` returns "insufficient history" forever and reads as 0% CAR. `--history ib` reads
the in-progress daily bar (a third clock convention). `minimum_order_margin_portfolio_percentage`
is inert for `market_order`. `blended_momentum` is dead code. The S-3 harness credits the
overnight gap that LEAN's next-open fill cannot capture.
