## 6. Standing methodology notes (carried, not new today)

- `sweep_s1.py` and LEAN disagree in **sign**, not just magnitude (S-10), and the sweep
  understates drawdown by ~2 points at champion size, ~6 on S-12's cell and ~8-10 at 2x
  exposure. Sweeps generate candidates; only LEAN decides, and a sweep rejection is grounds for
  one confirmation run rather than for closing an idea.
- Promotion needs a **shelf, not a spike**: neighbouring cells must agree in sign. S-13's 0.02
  cell passed every statistical gate and was still correctly refused on this rule.
- The commission floor at this account size is **2.1bps per unit of turnover** (S-3), before any
  spread, which is more than the entire gross edge a daily reversal sleeve could find.
