## S-2 (index-ETF opening-range breakout) - refused, and the backlog is now empty of mechanisms

- **The durable research lesson: an ORB study without its own fade control measures the stop, not
  the signal.** On SPY/QQQ/IWM, 2,687 sessions, the breakout shows gross +2.11 bps/trip - and the
  *fade* shows +0.76. Both signs cannot own a direction. Split it: `(brk + fade)/2` is **positive
  in all sixteen cells** (+0.33 to +1.43 bps) and is pure convexity - a stop plus a hold-to-close
  exit truncates losses in either direction, so a coin flip collects it. Only `(brk - fade)/2` is
  the signal, and it peaks at **+1.09 bps against a 3.65 bps round trip** and is negative in 6 of
  16 cells. **Always run the mirrored control, and always report the decomposition.**
- **Mirror the control properly.** The first implementation put an *absolute* midpoint stop on the
  faded side, so a faded short entered above the range was already through its stop and exited on
  its own entry bar 99.8% of the time - the control measured nothing. Anchor the stop as a risk
  unit measured **from the fill**, not as an absolute level, whenever a control inverts direction.
- **A landmine fixed**: `alpaca_data.py --splits` *replaced* the store's split table with only the
  symbols passed, and `--symbols` defaults to the 16-name universe while `--start` defaults to
  2024-01-01. A bare `--splits` rewrote a 60-symbol table as 16 - silently re-introducing exactly
  the per-share-commission defect A-10 spent an iteration fixing. It merges now. **Pattern to
  distrust: any writer whose argument default is narrower than the file it owns.**
- **Tooling fact**: `slippage_report.py` and anything else reading the parquet stores needs plain
  `python`, **not** `py -3.11` - the LEAN-side 3.11 has no pyarrow.
- **Runtime**: `intraday_backtest.py` on 3 symbols x ~1,000 sessions is ~11 minutes; budget ~35
  minutes for a three-regime confirmation.
- **State after this iteration**: the backlog holds **no open research item with a stated
